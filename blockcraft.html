BLOCKCRAFT

Mine, build and explore a fresh blocky world.
Touch: left stick moves, drag the right side to look, ⛏ breaks (hold), ▣ places, ⤒ jumps, ✈ toggles flying.
Keyboard: WASD, Space jump (double-tap to fly), Shift down, 1–9 or wheel to pick a block; with touch controls off, click breaks and right-click places.
